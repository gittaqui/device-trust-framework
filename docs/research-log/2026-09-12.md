## Baseline definition
The baseline preserves existing hard DENY gates and the aggregate 0.75 ALLOW threshold, then requires at least `k` of eight safe-is-high factors to meet a uniform per-factor threshold `q`. If the aggregate model would ALLOW but the quorum is not met, the policy returns STEP_UP rather than DENY.

The sweep evaluates `q in {0.60, 0.70, 0.75}` and `k in {5, 6, 7}`. These values are transparent exploratory choices and are not attributed to Bhattarai et al.

## Result
On the existing 50,000-row seeded synthetic population (`seed=20260903`):

- weak quorum settings (`k=5` or `k=6`) retain substantial compensation and produce 8.74%–12.48% unsafe false ALLOW rates;
- a 7-of-8 quorum sharply changes behavior;
- `q=0.70, k=7` produces 0.00% synthetic unsafe false ALLOW, 6.64% safe STEP_UP, and 0.00% safe DENY;
- `q=0.75, k=7` also produces 0.00% synthetic unsafe false ALLOW but increases safe STEP_UP to 8.20%;
- by comparison, the previously reported guarded 0.75 policy produces 1.24% false ALLOW and 5.40% safe STEP_UP under the same generator.

The numerical sweep was independently recomputed from the repository's seeded scenario definitions and trust equations before results were committed. A new unit-test module was added, but no claim is made here that the complete repository test suite was executed in this run.

## Interpretation
The result demonstrates that non-compensation need not depend on a signal-specific identity guard: a transparent evidence quorum can prevent high aggregate scores from masking multiple weak factors.

However, the favorable 7-of-8 result is also a warning about synthetic design alignment. The generator makes `identity_risk` and `adversarial_compliant` states weak on at least two safety-oriented factors, while healthy states are strong on nearly all factors and `policy_drift` is primarily weak on compliance. A 7-of-8 rule therefore matches the generator unusually well. The 0.00% synthetic false-ALLOW result must not be interpreted as production superiority.

## Limitations
- All outcome labels and feature distributions in this experiment are synthetic.
- The quorum structure is literature-inspired but the uniform factor thresholds and 7-of-8 setting are repository-specific exploratory choices.
- The experiment does not reproduce the AMI domain, attributes, thresholds, or evaluation of Bhattarai et al.
- The same synthetic generator has informed multiple model iterations, so design-overfitting remains material.
- No external telemetry was used and the external-validation claim firewall remains unchanged.
- The newly added unit tests were not executed as a full repository suite in this run.
