## Work
Created reproducible Python experiment `experiments/ar1_plugin_audit_2026_11_21.py` and six regression tests (included in accompanying downloadable research package). Compared naive known-unit-variance normal intervals, true-correlation oracle AR(1) intervals, and sample-autocorrelation plug-in intervals. Finite-sample variance multiplier: 1 + 2 sum((1-k/n)*phi^k, k=1,...,n-1). Stationary Gaussian AR(1), n=10/30/100, phi=0/.6/.9, 2,000 Monte Carlo datasets per cell, seed 20261121.
