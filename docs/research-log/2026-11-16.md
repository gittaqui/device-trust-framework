## Question and exact model
Does treating ten temporally ordered outage episodes as independent underestimate uncertainty? For stationary Gaussian AR(1) with correlation phi^k and marginal variance sigma^2, the variance of the ten-episode mean is sigma^2/10 times [1 + 2 sum(k=1..9) (1-k/10) phi^k]. The known-marginal-variance normal-reference 95% coverage if dependence is ignored is 2 Phi(1.96/sqrt(VIF))-1. This is an exact analytical model calculation, NOT Monte Carlo and NOT coverage of the November 15 Student-t interval, which estimates variance.
