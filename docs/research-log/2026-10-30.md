## Limitations and next step

Dropout is deliberately conditional on the underlying (possibly unobserved) synthetic signal value; all eight columns are affected. The scenario labels are constructed, not independently adjudicated, and three mask seeds are not independent enterprise samples. The study is exploratory and not preregistered; no real-world efficacy or calibrated confidence interval is claimed. Next: single-signal value-dependent ablations, authorized non-production endpoint evaluation with independent outcome adjudication, full repository tests, and full manuscript compilation. **Not IEEE submission-ready.**
