## Interpretation, limitations, next step

The identity assurance signal shows the largest of the three tested **single-signal** renormalization vulnerabilities. All-eight masking is substantially worse, but interactions and five other dimensions prevent additive attribution. Target selection and analysis followed previous outcome inspection, so this is exploratory, not preregistered. Labels and risk ranges are synthetic and jointly specified; three seeds do not provide population confidence intervals. No real-world missingness model is identified.

**Next:** independent, adjudicated non-production endpoint validation, with predeclared acceptable security/friction costs; correlated missingness stress testing; repository-wide tests and complete IEEE manuscript build. **Not IEEE submission-ready.**
