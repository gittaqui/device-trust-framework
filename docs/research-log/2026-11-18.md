## Interpretation
Small-sample mean-centered HAC normal-reference intervals may under-cover even at zero serial dependence, particularly with large bandwidth relative to episode count. Strong AR(1) persistence remains challenging at n=100. No general claims about HAC calibration follow from this fixed grid. Student-t, fixed-b, automatic bandwidth, block bootstrap, nonstationarity, cross-device dependence, and device-trust effectiveness were not evaluated. No confidential data used.
