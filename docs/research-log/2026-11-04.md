## Completed
Added standalone Python Monte Carlo right-censoring audit and four deterministic regression tests. The statistic of completed runs alone is downward biased for finite observation windows because long outages are more likely to be censored. Instead estimate missing-state exit probability q from all eligible missing->next transitions, then estimate mean duration as 1/q. Device starts with a missing run are included in transition likelihood, avoiding a run-start selection requirement. The final observation is excluded from transition exposure because its successor is unknown.

## Limitations and next step
The transition-based estimate assumes a stationary, homogeneous first-order Markov missingness process and complete visibility of the missingness indicator. A realistic telemetry collector may not observe transitions when the collector itself fails. Run length is measured in discrete intervals, not clock time. Next: integrate this estimator into the existing temporal generator and eight-factor access-decision evaluator, using paired seeds and device-cluster bootstrap; independently validate with approved non-production telemetry. No employer data used. Not IEEE submission-ready.
