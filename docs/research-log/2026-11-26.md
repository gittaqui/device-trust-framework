## Claim boundary and next experiment
Do not claim first multidimensional endpoint trust score, first explainable weighted score, or first sensitivity study. Candidate contribution is a reproducible evaluation of incremental value from explicit safety gates and evidence-quality handling under controlled endpoint scenarios, conditional on literature review and external validation.

The current baseline uses eight weighted factors, two hard gates and three decision bands. To isolate gate effects, compare binary compliance, the identical weighted score without gates, and the existing gated score, on the same rows with fixed thresholds. Report unsafe ALLOW, safe DENY, safe STEP_UP and unsafe STEP_UP separately; STEP_UP is not proof of successful containment. Include a matched safe-ALLOW operating-point comparison using separate development data, and report scenario-stratified counts. Treat missing evidence handling as a separate ablation rather than attributing all gains to dimensionality. Resource sensitivity is a future extension; hold resource class fixed for this comparison. Do not equate the simple weighted comparator with a reproduction of either cited paper.
