## Completed
Added a paired synthetic Monte Carlo comparison of episode-level Student-t intervals and sample-based Bartlett HAC (lag 8) normal-reference intervals. Stationary Gaussian AR(1) episodes with unit marginal variance, true mean zero, episode counts 10/30/100, phi 0/0.6/0.9, 1,000 datasets per condition, fixed seed 20261120. Both estimators are evaluated on the same generated datasets. Six local regression tests passed on an equivalent implementation; nine simulation conditions executed. The committed version was not independently executed in a clean checkout.
