## Contribution and prespecified model
Compare the prior naive device-level normal interval with an episode-level Student-t interval using ten independent episode means and the t(9) 97.5% critical value (2.2621571627409915). Synthetic model: Y_it = sqrt(rho) U_t + sqrt(1-rho) E_it, with independent standard Gaussian U_t and E_it. Truth is zero. 100 devices, 10 episodes, 2,000 independently generated datasets per condition, seed 20261115. Each episode mean averages 100 device observations. Independence of episode means, Gaussianity and equal episode sizes justify the exact t reference distribution in this toy model; this is **not** a general two-way-cluster estimator.

Monte Carlo standard error at 95% coverage is about 0.49 percentage points for 2,000 datasets. These results measure **statistical interval coverage under a synthetic Gaussian model**, not endpoint trust accuracy or real-world security effectiveness.

## Limitations and next step
Only 10 independent Gaussian episodes, no serial correlation, device random effects, heterogeneous episode sizes, missing telemetry, binary decisions, or endpoint measurements. The episode-t method cannot be assumed valid when episode means are serially dependent or non-Gaussian, and episode-only clustering may omit persistent device dependence. Next: introduce serial correlation and crossed device random effects; compare two-way cluster variance and episode block methods with coverage diagnostics, then integrate validated inference into the eight-factor engine.
