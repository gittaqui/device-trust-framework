## Design
Independently generate K synthetic device-level paired effects Uniform(-0.4,0.4), true population effect zero. Weights fixed at 50 or sampled independently from {10,20,50,100}. Compare nominal 95% percentile device-bootstrap (399 resamples) and cluster-linearized t intervals (K-1 degrees of freedom), using 300 independent datasets per condition and seed 20261113.
