## Evidence boundary and limitations
All figures are from a controlled **synthetic generator**. They are not observed enterprise false-allow rates, validated endpoint risk scores, or independent evidence of effectiveness. Pairing applies across strategies within each missingness regime, not across the two regimes. The critical-signal-biased mask is a stylized outage, not a validated MNAR mechanism. The renderer validates internal JSON consistency, not whether upstream synthetic observations were generated honestly or whether real endpoint labels are valid.

The full repository test suite, full IEEE manuscript build, and independent eight-factor endpoint evaluation remain unverified. The manuscript's separate LANL second-cohort status text remains outdated and requires a dedicated evidence-checked update.
