## Results
With zero benign identity overlap, guarded 0.80 minimized normalized loss when STEP_UP cost was 0.025 or 0.05; guarded 0.75 minimized loss when STEP_UP cost was 0.10 or 0.20. Under 100% benign reinterpretation of the synthetic `identity_risk` region, additive 0.80 became preferred for STEP_UP costs 0.025/0.05, while additive 0.75 became preferred for 0.10/0.20.

The result is therefore a sensitivity finding rather than a production recommendation: policy ranking depends on both verification cost and how much benign behavior occupies the identity/anomaly region targeted by the guard.

## Limitations
All reported losses remain conditional on synthetic feature distributions, synthetic labels, and hypothetical normalized costs. The experiment does not estimate real enterprise verification burden or incident-loss ratios. Because the synthetic generator has informed model development, design-overfitting remains a threat to validity.

## Next step
Before adding more policy variants, the highest-value next step is to obtain or process externally sourced temporally ordered host/authentication telemetry and freeze a mapping from external events to the already defined proxy signals. If external telemetry is still unavailable, add a preregistered synthetic-to-external transition protocol specifying which policy parameters must remain frozen and which may be calibrated only on a training interval.
