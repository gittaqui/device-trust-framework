## Contribution
Added `experiments/conditional_bootstrap_coverage_2026_11_10.py`, a deterministic Monte Carlo coverage audit of the *committed estimator's algorithm*. Each of K independent synthetic unsafe devices has latent paired ALLOW difference 1 with probability 0.3 and 0 otherwise. Arm 0 ALLOW count is zero and arm 1 count is either zero or the device's full interval count. True superpopulation interval-weighted effect is 0.3 because interval length is independent of latent type. Two designs: fixed 20 observations per device; or 10/20/40 observations drawn independently. K=2,5,20,100. For each condition: 300 independent datasets and 399 percentile bootstrap replicates per dataset; fixed documented seeds. Coverage means the percentile 95% interval contains 0.3 (inclusive). Monte Carlo standard error is sqrt(p_hat(1-p_hat)/300). This is a deliberately discrete stress test, not a general coverage guarantee.

## Limitations and next step
The synthetic two-point effects are extreme and do not represent real device telemetry. No within-device stochasticity, common infrastructure episodes, or independently adjudicated labels. Bootstrap percentile intervals should not be represented as calibrated at sparse K.
