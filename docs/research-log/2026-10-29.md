## Limitations and next step

The structured missingness process is column-specific and **does not depend on signal values**; it is not an empirical MNAR mechanism. The synthetic labels are deterministic by scenario and not independent ground truth. No inferential statistics or external performance claims are made. Next: pre-register a value-dependent/MNAR sensitivity design and investigate non-compensatory identity assurance, followed by independently labeled lab validation. The manuscript is not IEEE-ready.
