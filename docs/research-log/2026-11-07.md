## Limitations
The code treats devices as independent resampling units and labels as fixed. Common-cause infrastructure outages require episode-level clustering. The bootstrap percentile interval is conditional on the synthetic sample-generating assumptions and may be unstable with few unsafe devices. Device posture remains static in the proposed longitudinal stress test. No confidential employer data was accessed.
